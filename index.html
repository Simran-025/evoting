How the System Worksप्रणाली कैसे कार्य करती है

1

Aadhaar Identity Verification via UIDAI API

Your 12-digit Aadhaar and OTP are verified. A SHA-3 commitment is computed locally and your Aadhaar is immediately discarded.

UIDAI APISHA-3OTP Auth
2

Voter Nullifier — One-Person One-Vote

A unique Voter Nullifier derived via Poseidon hashing is stored on-chain. It prevents double-voting without ever revealing your identity.

Poseidon HashNullifierAnti-Double-Vote
3

Zero-Knowledge Proof — Groth16/BN254

A zk-SNARK proof C(x,w)=1 proves you are eligible and haven't voted — without revealing your identity or vote choice to anyone.

Groth16zk-SNARKC(x,w)=1
4

IPFS Sharding + Blockchain Recording

Anonymised metadata is sharded across IPFS nodes. The nullifier and proof are submitted to the smart contract which atomically records your vote.

IPFSSolidity 0.8.24Ethereum PoS
